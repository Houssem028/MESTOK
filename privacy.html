Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

MesTok is a messaging companion project. This page explains, in general terms, how the app is designed to handle information. Features and data handling may change as the product is developed.

Information and permissions

Depending on the version and the features you choose to use, MesTok may process basic profile information returned by TikTok Login Kit, such as your TikTok display name, profile picture and account identifier. Android versions may request notification access so the app can display notifications that Android makes available to it. You can manage or revoke Android permissions in your device settings.

TikTok access

TikTok Login Kit with the user.info.basic permission provides basic profile information. It does not grant MesTok access to private TikTok direct messages. MesTok does not claim that this permission enables reading or sending TikTok DMs. Any messaging feature will be offered only where a supported integration and the necessary permissions make it available.

Use and sharing

Information is used to provide and improve the features you choose to use. MesTok does not sell personal information. Do not enter passwords, verification codes or other sensitive account credentials into MesTok. MesTok does not ask you to share your TikTok password.

Storage and security

The exact information stored depends on the app version and enabled features. We aim to limit collection to what is necessary and use reasonable safeguards. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party services

MesTok may rely on third-party services, including TikTok authentication. Their processing of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. MesTok is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

Your choices

You may stop using MesTok, disconnect an account where that option is available, and revoke Android permissions through device settings. You may also uninstall the app.

Contact

This project page does not currently publish a dedicated privacy contact address. Do not send passwords or verification codes when seeking support.
